## What would flip a no

- Proof from my own phone that I'm actually losing calls I never get back to, say a month of missed-call numbers that never rang again, plus a free trial with no setup fee.
- A colleague from the Innung telling me he's used it for half a year and it actually brought him jobs, with no setup fee so I can just try it and quit.
- If it sorted out and turned away the jobs I can't take, and answered existing customers about appointments, so it saved me phone time instead of bringing in more enquiries. And a setup under 300 € with no long setup project.
- No setup fee and around 30-40 € a month, or proof from another one-man trade business that it actually won them jobs they would have lost.
- A tradesman I know personally using it for a few months and telling me it works and didn't mess with his number.
- If it could politely tell callers we're fully booked and sort out only my existing regular customers' urgent jobs, with no setup fee and nothing to manage myself.
- If it sorted and turned down the enquiries for me, filtering out small jobs and tyre-kickers so my office spent less time on the phone, and it came from a company with other trade businesses already using it, at a much lower monthly price.
- If landline callers got a spoken message and could leave their request by voice, and it cost under 50 a month with no setup fee and a free month to prove it pays off.
- A free month on my own number, set up in an afternoon, with references from other one-man shops nearby, and around 49 a month with no big setup fee.
- A free month or no setup fee until it works, plus a couple of trade businesses nearby I can call who use it.
- If my office help left and nobody was answering the phone anymore, and a few trade colleagues I know had it running for a year without trouble.
- If it filtered and politely put off non-urgent enquiries for me, so it saved my office time instead of creating more callbacks, at a much lower price with no setup fee.
