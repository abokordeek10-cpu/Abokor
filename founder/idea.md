# Abora Automation

- What it is: A two-founder service business in Viernheim (Germany) that sets up and runs AI automations for the office work of small trade businesses (Handwerksbetriebe): answering and pre-sorting customer enquiries, later booking appointments, drafting quotes, following up on open quotes and asking for reviews.
- Who it is for: Owners of small and medium trade businesses in Germany, all trades (plumbing/heating, electrical, roofing, painting, carpentry, landscaping, tiling, drywall). They work on site all day, do office work in the evening, have little time and little interest in technology.
- What it sells, at what price: One-time setup + monthly service. Actively sold today: "Start" = enquiries + pre-sorting (website form and e-mail), 990 € setup + 149 €/month; pilot price for the first 3 businesses: 495 € setup. On request only (not yet delivered): "Business" 1.990 € + 249 €/month (adds appointments + follow-up), "Komplett" from 3.990 € + from 399 €/month (all six modules). Small-business VAT exemption (§ 19 UStG), so prices are final. Monthly cancellable.
- Where and how: Sold across Germany, first pilots ideally within ~30 km of Viernheim (Rhein-Neckar). Customers are found through cold phone calls to businesses, TikTok/Instagram "build in public" videos, the website abora-automation.de and personal network. Delivered remotely: own server in Germany (Hetzner), automation tool n8n, AI model Claude (Anthropic). Setup usually 2–4 weeks.
- Founders' observation (16 Nov 2026): most enquiries to trade businesses arrive by phone call, not by form or e-mail. The Start package is therefore being rebuilt around missed calls.
- Budget and constraints: Two founders (Abokor Deek Osman, Ali Reza Ansari), 0 paying customers, 0 relevant followers, no outside funding known. Founders are self-taught; one live demo exists. No cost calculation behind prices yet. Goal: 3 paying pilot businesses by 31 January 2027. Long-term vision: own automation platform for other industries, later industrial customers.
