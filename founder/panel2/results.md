## What would flip a no

- If the free 14-day check showed in black and white that real new customers rang, never got through and never called again, enough jobs to cover the fee many times over, I might try the 30 free days.
- A colleague from the Innung or a trade I know telling me he's used it for months and it actually brought him jobs without annoying his customers.
- If the free 14-day check showed regular customers or emergencies were actually getting lost, and the ausgebucht mode really kept everything else off my phone, I might try it.
- If the free 14-day check showed me in black and white that several callers never rang back and those were real jobs worth more than the fee, I'd think about it, but at a lower price.
- A fellow trade business owner I know telling me they've used it for a few months without problems.
- If the 'ausgebucht' mode alone could keep everyone except emergencies and my regulars away from me, with zero setup on my side and no forwarding fiddling, I might try the free month.
- If my office help retired or left and nobody was there to pick up, the ausgebucht mode that filters to emergencies and regular customers could be worth it, ideally from a company that has been around a few years.
- If the free 14-day check showed real jobs being lost every week, enough that one saved job a month clearly pays the fee. Even then I'd want it closer to 49 euros.
- If the free check shows I'm really losing several paying jobs a week, and another Handwerker nearby I know has used it for a few months without problems.
- Another plumber or heating firm near me who has used it for a few months and tells me it never dropped a call. Then I'd run the free 14-day check.
- If the free check showed regular customers or emergencies actually slipping through and getting lost, I might switch on the ausgebucht mode.
- If the free check showed regular customers or emergency jobs getting lost, not just new enquiries, I might try the 'ausgebucht' mode to filter calls.
