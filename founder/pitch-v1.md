Abora Automation from Viernheim, for trade businesses (Handwerksbetriebe).
When you can't take a call, the caller automatically gets an SMS or WhatsApp within seconds: "Thanks for calling <your business>. We're on a job and will call you back shortly. Reply here with a short text or voice message saying what it's about." You keep your own number; missed calls are simply forwarded.
Their reply (text, photo or voice note) arrives on your phone as a short summary with the urgency marked, plus an overview of all enquiries every evening. Website and e-mail enquiries are included.
