# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The market is mostly right.** Real pain (enquiries arrive while the owner is on a roof and get answered at 9 p.m. or never) and buyers with money. Weak spots: they are hard to reach (on site all day, little interest in tech), and nothing shows the market is growing.
- **Low effort for the buyer.** Done for them, remote, 2–4 weeks, owner approves every price. Effort and time already score well.
- **Honest rules build trust.** No invented testimonials, AI says it is AI, owner stays in control. This helps likelihood with buyers who distrust tech.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is close to zero today.** No customers, no case study, one demo, one cold call. A trade owner has no reason to believe 990 € buys a result.
2. **The package being sold is the weakest one.** "Start" sorts form and e-mail enquiries. The dream outcome is *more jobs won and less evening paperwork*. Following up on open quotes (the module closest to money) and quote drafts are only sold on request and not built yet. The buyer is asked to pay for a tidier inbox, not for more revenue.
3. **It is priced like a commodity.** 149 €/month sits inside the 80–400 € range the founders found for agencies. With no guarantee, no named outcome and no stack, it competes on price with no cost model behind that price.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In euros, what is one missed or slow enquiry worth to a typical plumbing or roofing business? (/founder-consumer)
- What does one customer cost you per month in server, AI use and your own hours? (/founder-cfo)
- What does it cost you, in calls and hours, to win one pilot? (/founder-cfo, after you track the pilots)
- Who else sells this to trades, and do they offer a guarantee? (/founder-competitors)
- If owners' enquiries come by phone or WhatsApp, why are those "on request"?
- Could you charge twice as much if you promised "every enquiry answered in 5 minutes, or the month is free"?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Rebuild "Start" around one result you can measure (e.g. every enquiry answered within minutes plus automatic follow-up on open quotes) and stack in the channel owners actually use (WhatsApp/phone) and a weekly summary.
2. Add a guarantee you can afford (e.g. setup fee back if the response-time result is not hit in 60 days), costed with /founder-cfo before you offer it.
3. Treat the 3 pilots as proof generators: measure response time and quotes won before and after, and get permission to publish the numbers.
4. Do not sell "Business"/"Komplett" until Start has 3 documented results.
