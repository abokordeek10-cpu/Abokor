# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **There is a one-sentence pitch.** "Du machst das Handwerk. Wir machen den Rest." The job is clear: no enquiry gets lost while the owner is on site.
- **The sold product is narrow.** "Start" (enquiries plus pre-sorting, form and e-mail) is the right size for a first product. Modules 3–6 stay off the price list.
- **The demo starts from the experience.** The owner picks his trade, types an enquiry and sees the reply. That is a real first five minutes.
- **The trust rules are good product decisions.** The AI discloses itself and the owner approves every price.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The one thing may cover the wrong channel.** Start handles forms and e-mail. Phone and WhatsApp are "on request". If most trade enquiries arrive by phone or WhatsApp, Start fixes the smallest share of the pain, and owners will cancel a monthly service. Nobody has measured the channel split (/founder-consumer can estimate it; pilot data will settle it).
2. **Seams nobody is watching.** The chain is mailbox, then n8n, then Claude, then a reply in the owner's name. The owner is on a roof and not technical. If a connection breaks or a reply comes out wrong, he hears about it from an angry customer. No monitoring, alerts or support promise is described. Under a "we do the rest" promise, one silent failure costs the customer's trust.
3. **Focus is leaking.** Two founders with 0 customers are spread across six modules, a phone agent, a research agent, two social channels, cold calls and a platform vision. Because modules 3–6 are "built with pilot customers", the first references get a half-built product.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- At 7 pm after go-live, what does a pilot owner see on his phone, and what does he tap?
- What share of a trade business's enquiries arrive by form or e-mail, and how do you know?
- When the automation fails at 10 am, who notices first?
- What does onboarding ask of the owner (mailbox access, website access, minutes of his time)?
- What will you stop building until three pilots are live?
- What does one Start customer cost to serve each month? (/founder-cfo)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Build and sell only Start until 3 pilots have run 60 days with no silent failure. Park the phone agent, modules 3–6 and the platform until then.
2. Before the first pilot goes live, add error alerts, a daily check that each connection is alive, and a written response-time promise.
3. Measure each pilot's enquiry channels in week 1. If phone or WhatsApp dominates, make that channel the core of Start.
4. Script the owner's first five minutes after go-live and test it with one real owner.
5. Get the cost to serve from /founder-cfo before you confirm the 149 €/month price.
