Abora Automation from Viernheim, for trade businesses (Handwerksbetriebe).
Free first: a 14-day "missed call check". We count how many callers rang you while you were busy and never called again. You see your own numbers before you pay anything.
Then "Kein Anruf verloren": you keep your own number; calls you can't take are forwarded. Callers on a mobile get an SMS or WhatsApp within seconds ("We're on a job and will call you back today. Reply with a text, photo or voice note saying what it's about"); landline callers hear a short recorded greeting in your name and can leave a message. Every request reaches your phone as a short summary, emergencies first, plus an evening overview.
Fully booked? Switch on "ausgebucht" mode: callers are told politely you have no free dates right now; only emergencies and your regular customers come through.
No setup fee. First 30 days free, then 99 € per month, cancellable monthly, no VAT. If you cancel, we remove the forwarding the same day.
A new company with two founders and no customers yet; there is a demo you can call yourself.
